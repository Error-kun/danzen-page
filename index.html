Danzen

The NEXT Best App For Anime & Manga

Danzen is a fork of Dartotsu.

Inspired by Saikou, Dantotsu, and ReDantotsu.

Features

Features

  • Lists and trackers

    Anime, manga, and novels with AniList, MyAnimeList, Simkl, Kitsu, MangaBaka, and MangaUpdates.

  • Search and discovery

    Trending shelves, filters, and ways to find what to watch next.

  • Schedules

    Release calendars and reminders when new episodes land.

  • Social

    Profiles, feeds, stories, groups, and comments on titles.

  • Themes

    Built-in palettes, glass cards, and layout you can tune.

  • Downloads

    Queue and manage files on your device.

  • Extensions

    Add third-party sources. Extensions are not shipped by Danzen.

  • Discord presence

    Show friends what you are watching or reading.

  • Cross-platform

    Android, Windows, Linux, macOS, and iOS.
