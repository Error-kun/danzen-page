Features

  • Trackers

    AniList, MyAnimeList, and other trackers in one place.

  • Downloads

    Queue and manage downloads on device.

  • Themes

    Colors, cards, and layout you can change.

  • Discord RPC

    Show what you are watching or reading.

  • Cross-platform

    Android, Windows, Linux, macOS, and iOS.

  • Extensions

    Add sources with extensions. Extensions are third-party.
