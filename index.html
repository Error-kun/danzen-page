Danzen

Track and manage your anime and manga lists in one place.

Danzen is a fork of Dartotsu.

Features

Lists

Manage your anime and manga lists across supported tracking services, with optional cross-account syncing.

Discovery

Explore trending titles, browse popular picks, and search with filters.

Schedule

Follow release schedules and get notified when new episodes arrive.

Personalization

Customize themes, colors, and card styles to match how you browse.
